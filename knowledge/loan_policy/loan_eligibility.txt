LOAN ELIGIBILITY POLICY

Credit History
A positive credit history is an important factor considered during loan assessment.
A negative or unavailable credit history may reduce the likelihood of loan approval.
Credit history is considered together with other applicant information.

Applicant Income
Applicant income is considered when assessing a loan application.
Higher income does not automatically guarantee loan approval.
Income should be evaluated together with other application characteristics.

Coapplicant Income
Coapplicant income may contribute to the assessment of the applicant's financial situation.
It does not independently determine whether a loan will be approved.

Loan Amount
The requested loan amount is considered during loan assessment.
A higher requested amount does not automatically result in rejection or approval.

Loan Term
The requested loan term is considered as part of the loan application.
The loan term alone does not determine the final decision.

Education
Education information may be considered as part of the applicant profile.
Education alone does not determine loan approval.

Marital Status
Marital status may be included as applicant information during assessment.
Marital status alone does not determine loan approval.

Dependents
The number of dependents may be considered as part of the applicant profile.
The number of dependents alone does not determine loan approval.

Property Area
Property area may be included as part of the applicant information.
Property area alone does not determine loan approval.

Self Employment
Self-employment status may be considered as part of the applicant profile.
Self-employment status alone does not determine loan approval.

Important
The machine learning model in this project produces the loan prediction.
The policy information in this document provides general context and must not override the machine learning model's prediction.
